EDGE LEDGER - DESKTOP VERSION
=============================

Your journal now lives on your laptop. You need Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.


1. PUT THE APP SOMEWHERE SAFE
   - Make a folder called "Edge Ledger" in Documents.
   - Put "Edge Ledger.html" inside it.


2. OPEN IT
   - Right-click "Edge Ledger.html", choose Open with, then Google Chrome.
   - Click "Choose my folder".
   - In the window that opens, go to Documents > Edge Ledger, click "New folder",
     name it "Edge Ledger Data", and select it.
   - When Chrome asks, allow it to save changes. Pick "Allow on every visit" if it's offered.

   Your trades, journal, screenshots and news are saved in "Edge Ledger Data" as normal files.
   Chrome remembers the folder. If it ever asks again, click "Open my journal".


3. MAKE IT FEEL LIKE AN APP (optional)
   - Bookmark it: press Ctrl+D (Cmd+D on Mac) while it's open.
   - Or pin the tab: right-click the tab and choose Pin.


4. BRING OVER YOUR TRADES FROM EDGE LEDGER ONLINE
   - Open Edge Ledger on claude.ai, go to Settings, and click "Export for desktop".
     Save the .zip file.
   - In the desktop app, go to Settings and click "Restore or move in from a file".
     Pick the .zip.
   - Your trades, journal, playbooks, accounts, news log and screenshots all come across.


5. COACH AND AUTOMATIC NEWS (optional, needs a Claude API key)
   - Go to console.anthropic.com, sign in, add some credit, and create an API key.
   - In Edge Ledger, go to Settings > Claude, paste the key, and click Save key.
   - Costs: a coach answer is a few cents, and a day's news log is roughly 10 to 30 cents.
   - Turn on "Log each weekday's market news automatically" if you want it done for you
     after the New York close while the app is open.
   - Red-folder USD events from the free ForexFactory calendar show without a key.


BRINGING IN YOUR OLD JOURNAL (OneNote and Notion)
   - Trades > Import trades, then pick OneNote or Notion at the top.
     (Or Journal > "Bring in my old journal".)
   - OneNote: choose "OneNote journal.txt", check the list, then click Bring in.
   - Notion: choose "Notion export_all.csv", check the preview, then click Bring in.
   - Old trades go into a closed account called "Past trades (old journal)".
   - Running it again is safe: anything already brought in is skipped.


BACKUPS
   - Edge Ledger keeps one copy of your journal per day in "Edge Ledger Data/backups"
     (the last 30 days).
   - To back up everything, including screenshots, copy the whole "Edge Ledger Data" folder
     to a USB drive.
   - Or keep the folder inside Google Drive or OneDrive on your computer so it's backed up
     automatically.

Only open Edge Ledger in one tab at a time.
