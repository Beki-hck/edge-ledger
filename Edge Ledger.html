Import trades

      

      

        
Export your closed trades from your platform, then upload the file or paste the table here. Trades go into the account selected at the top.

        
    
          
  • MT5: History tab, right-click, Report, save as Excel. Open it, copy the Positions table including its header row, and paste it below.

    • 
          
  • cTrader: History tab, export to CSV or Excel. Upload the CSV, or copy the table and paste it.

Match the columns

          

        

        

      

      

        
Brings in your OneNote trading journal. Each page becomes a day in the journal with its thoughts and its good and bad points, and each P/L becomes a trade. Nothing is saved until you check the list and click Bring in.

        
    
          
  • In OneNote, print your trading journal section to PDF.
    • 
          
  • Open the PDF in Chrome or Edge, press Ctrl+A and then Ctrl+C, and paste it below. If you have the notebook as a .txt file, choose that instead.

Pictures from OneNote

          
The pictures from your OneNote PDF come in through the same picker as Notion's. Choose the "OneNote pictures" folder there, and each picture goes on its day under Charts.

          

        

      

      

        
Brings in a Notion trading journal. Each row with a P/L becomes a trade, and rows without one are saved as notes on their day.

  • Unzip the download and choose the .csv file inside. If there are two, choose the one ending in _all.

Backup and moving in

      
Download everything except screenshots as one file, or bring in a backup. To move from Edge Ledger online, open it on claude.ai, go to Settings and click Export for desktop, then pick that .zip file here. Your trades, journal, playbooks, accounts, news log and screenshots all come across.
